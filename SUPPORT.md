# Getting help

## Is this my problem?

Read [docs/A52S_CALL_AUDIO_ISSUE.md](docs/A52S_CALL_AUDIO_ISSUE.md). It lists every known variant of the Galaxy A52s 5G Bluetooth call-audio failure, affected firmware and the community workarounds.

## Where to go

| You want to … | Go to |
|---|---|
| Report a call that failed (with or without the app) | [Call-audio problem](https://github.com/KaiPressmar/a52s-bluetooth-repair/issues/new?template=1-call-audio-problem.yml) |
| Tell us how the app works in your car | [Field report](https://github.com/KaiPressmar/a52s-bluetooth-repair/issues/new?template=2-field-report.yml) |
| Ask a question or share an idea | [Discussions](https://github.com/KaiPressmar/a52s-bluetooth-repair/discussions) |
| Report a security issue | [Private advisory](https://github.com/KaiPressmar/a52s-bluetooth-repair/security/advisories/new) |

## Before you report

1. When the fault happens, **don't reboot yet**. Open the app → *Einstellungen* → *Diagnosebericht teilen* and save the file.
2. Note the firmware (*Settings → About phone → Software information → Build number*) and your car or headset model.
3. Remove phone numbers, names and Bluetooth addresses before posting.

This is a volunteer project; answers may take a few days. German and English are both fine.
