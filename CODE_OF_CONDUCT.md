# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

- Be respectful and patient. Many reporters are frustrated users with a broken phone, not developers.
- Assume good intent, stay on topic and give constructive, evidence-based feedback.
- Protect privacy: never post other people's personal data, phone numbers or device identifiers.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or sexualized content.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to issues, discussions, pull requests and any other project space. Maintainers may edit or remove content and block participants who violate it. Report problems privately to the maintainer via a [private advisory](https://github.com/KaiPressmar/a52s-bluetooth-repair/security/advisories/new) or the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile. All reports are handled confidentially.
